2. The app collects no data

Nail Craft has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet. The app itself sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else. It asks for no permissions: it does not use your camera, photos, contacts or location.

Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

3. Your designs stay on your device

The app saves the following only in its own private storage on your device:

DataWhat it is
Favorites The designs you save, with a picture of each one
Design in progress The design you are working on, so you can continue it later
Settings Your default skin tone and which tips you have already seen
Today's inspiration The design suggested for today, so it stays the same all day

None of this is stored on a server or accessible to the developer. There is no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

4. Device backups

If backups are turned on in your device settings, Android or iOS may include the app's data in your own backup. The developer has no access to it.

5. Sharing designs

When you share a design, the app hands a picture of it to the app you choose in your device's share menu. The developer does not receive it.

6. Contacting support by email

The Support screen opens your own email app with the address nailcraft.support@gmail.com and a subject already filled in. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

If you email us, your message, your email address and any details you include are processed to answer you. The legal basis is legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Messages are deleted once the matter is settled and no retention obligation applies.

Recipient: the support mailbox is a Gmail account, so your email is stored by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC in the United States.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data erased (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21).

To exercise any of these, email the address in section 1. The developer holds no app data about you, since everything the app saves stays on your device.
